💜 Your Safety Matters

✅ You do not have to reply to hurtful messages.
✅ Tell a parent, teacher, or another trusted adult.
✅ Block or report accounts that threaten or harass you.
✅ Never share intimate pictures with anyone who pressures you.
✅ Keep threatening text messages as evidence when it is safe, but do not download or forward intimate images of children.
🚨 In India, call 112 in an emergency. Children can seek support through Child Helpline 1098.

🔐 Privacy First

SafeSpace checks messages directly in your browser. It does not send messages to a server.

This is a rule-based educational prototype. It is not a trained AI model and cannot guarantee that a message is safe.
